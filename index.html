How Schedulr Works

No accounts. No servers. Everything travels inside your links so nobody has to read 20 texts to book one session.

1
DM sets the dates

Go to 👑 DM Setup, pick your dates, and copy the Invite Link to your group chat.

2
Players tap 3 buttons

Players open the link, enter their name, and tap 🔥 DTF, 🎲 Maybe, or 💀 Pass for each date (no 5-paragraph texts allowed).

3
Pick the winning night

Players send back their Reply Link. Opening (or pasting) it adds them to 🟨 The Grid and highlights the best night to play.
